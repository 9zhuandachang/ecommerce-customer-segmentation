# CDNOW Online Retail Transactions: Fixed cross-merchant checks before downloading

Logging before downloading full source files, reading client-level tags or model results. The source is [CDNOW complete data](https://www.brucehardie.com/notes/026/) released by the original researcher Bruce Hardie; this page description contains the complete purchase history of 23,570 customers who first purchased in the first quarter of 1997 to the end of 6 in year 1998. Use the original `CDNOW_master.zip`, not the tenth example. The original compressed package is only placed in the temporary directory of the operating system, and the warehouse only retains the aggregation results, source URL, SHA- 256 and audit.

## Transaction and time definition

- Each row of the original file is a transaction, and the four columns are the customer ID, purchase date, number of CDs purchased, and the total dollar amount of the transaction. The customer ID and date must be valid; the quantity and amount must be greater than zero and the amount must be accurate to cents. Keep each line in the source file, including multiple transactions of the same customer with the same amount on the same day; use the source line serial number plus the customer ID as the unique invoice ID, and do not merge orders by date. Record all exclusions and amount reconciliations. If the date exceeds the 1997 - 01 - 01 to 1998 - 06 - 30 specified by the source, stop and audit.
- The file does not have a product ID or product breakdown. Fix `stock_code` for all transactions to `CD`, so that the product diversity characteristics of the original project are always 1; no forgery of product categories is allowed. The semantics of this dimension are different from the original merchant, and the results are only used for cross-merchant method testing.
- Establish a cut-off point on the 1 day of each calendar month from 1997 - 07 - 01 to 1998 - 06 - 01, enter `[t-180d,t)` customers with transactions, and target the total real transaction dollar amount of `[t,t+30d)`. If the source date is not large enough to cover the complete input or 30 days in the future, this cutoff is not evaluated.
- 1997 months 7 – 9 months are only for initial training queue; 10 – 12 months are for rolling development inspection; 1998 months 1 – 3 months are for development confirmation; **1998 months 4 – 6 are the final inspection for this lock**. Each target month is only trained with earlier cutoffs for which full labeling has ended; scoring is started only after at least three mature cutoffs. In the test month, there must be at least 1,000 customer snapshots and 100 future buyers every month, otherwise only the feasibility will be reported and the model will not be judged. Calibers or parameters cannot be changed based on the last three months.

## Fixed methods and indicators

- The model maintains the original studied 20 customer monthly characteristics and LightGBM Tweedie: 120 tree, learning rate 0.05, 15 leaves, variance power 1.5, seed 42. Product diversity on CDNOW is always 1. The comparison is the amount `/6` in the past 180 days and the amount `/3` in the past 90 days. All methods use the same client and cutoff point.
- In addition, it is fixed to compare the existing mature historical lag amount calibration of the original project: the current original model prediction is multiplied by up to three labels of the complete mature cut-off point of `Total actual amount/Total forecast amount`, and the multiplication rate is limited to `[0.7,1.3]` and then shrinks to 1 by half. Only past out-of-sample predictions and mature labels are used; no current or future results are read. Report the original model and calibration version, and do not select the best and re-run based on the test results.
- Combined reports for all MAE, actual buyers MAE, and monthly forecast Top 20 customers' actual consumption coverage, total deviation, Top 20 group deviation, zero amount ratio, and training label cutoff on a monthly basis and in the last three months; when merging, select Top 20 each month. Completely save source file hash, exclude audit, snapshot number, label and 180 day amount check. Original reinforcement target ( MAE at least low 10%, buyers MAE low 5%, Top 20 high 3 percentage points, total deviation ± 5%, Top 20 group deviation ± 10%, three months by month MAE and Top 20 are not bad) are displayed side by side as they are without changing the threshold afterwards.

This is real but old online music retail data that is limited to new buyers in the first quarter. Neither passing nor passing can pretend to be an independent acceptance by the original UK merchant in the new month; it only tests whether the method is stable in another online merchant with real transaction amounts and repeat purchases. USD amounts do not add up to GBP amounts.
