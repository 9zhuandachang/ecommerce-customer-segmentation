# DataCo Real Transaction: Cross-Merchant Fixed Testing for Dual Output Method

Recorded before downloading the full transaction file, viewing client-level future tags or model results for this data. Raw data published by the DataCo research team at [Mendeley Data v 5](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/8gx2fvg2k6/5) (DOI 10.17632 / 8 gx 2 fvg 2 k 6.5, CC BY 4.0 ). If the official document cannot be obtained directly through AP I, you can download the public image of Kaggle; you need to keep the download URL, compressed package SHA- 256, file name, original line number and field verification. The original files only remain in the local system temporary directory, and the warehouse does not save original customer lines, personal information, or customer-level external forecasts. Only the customer ID, order ID, line ID, order date, product ID, quantity, line amount after discount, order status and other fields required to complete this experiment are read. Name, email, address or password are not read.

## Sources and Windows

- Qualified transaction lines must have customer ID, order ID, product ID, valid order date, positive quantity, positive `Order Item Total`, and the final order status is `COMPLETE` or `CLOSED` (case insensitive). Other statuses and missing and non-positive amount lines are excluded and counted based on mutually exclusive main reasons. The line amount is rounded to cents according to the source value, and the total amount before and after rounding is recorded; multiple product lines of the same order are kept separately, and the invoice frequency is calculated based on the unique order ID. If the same order ID corresponds to multiple customers, or the same row ID conflicts, stop and audit. `Order Item Total` is the row amount after discount; do not treat future summary columns such as `Sales per customer` as input.
- Automatically generate the monthly 1 cut-off point from the actual earliest and latest days of eligible transactions, input window `[t-180d,t)`, future amount and purchase label as `[t,t+30d)`. Only keep months in which both 180 day observations and 30 day labels are complete; if coverage is discontinuous, there are less than 12 complete cutoffs, or the last label ends beyond the end of the file, stop and only report feasibility. Dates are not selected by model results.
- In chronological order, the last 3 complete cutoff points are **locked testing**, the previous 3 ones are development confirmations, and the rest are earlier history. Only the mature label of `training cutoff+30day<=Rating cutoff` is used for each scoring cutoff. If there are less than 1,000 customer snapshots with transactions in the previous 180 days or less than 100 future buyers in any month of the lock-in test, only the feasibility will be reported and the model will not be judged.

## Fixed predictor and two business outputs

1. **Expected amount of all customers**:  Reuse stage five  A  group  8  feature  LightGBM  Repeat purchase classifier  (15  leaf, 64  Tree,  learning rate  0.05,  class balance,  seed  42),  Each discount is fitted in the earlier month with fully observed labels,  Keep the most recent three months with fully observed labels for independent calibration queues,  Only use the last two months to do the frozen classifier  sigmoid  Probability calibration. The average amount after purchase will be used separately  20  Characteristics before the term cutoff point  Gamma LightGBM,  Fit on all mature historical buyers  (120  Tree, 0.05, 15  leaf,  seed  42). The expected amount is equal to the calibrated purchase probability multiplied by the conditional mean,  Not using true future purchase status. History with the same customer  180  Amount per day  `/6`, 20  Feature direct  Tweedie (120  Tree, 0.05, 15  leaf,  power  1.5,  seed  42) Compare.
2. **If the amount after purchase**: 20 features LightGBM L 1 conditional median, only fitted on mature historical buyers ( 120 tree, 0.05, 15 leaves, seeds 42 ). This is a conditional scenario forecast and cannot be used as an unconditional amount for all customers; it is only evaluated for future actual buyers and compared with `past180Amount per day/6` and `past180Average order amount per day` at the same time. When forecasting you don't know who will buy.

## Fixed reports and criteria in advance

- Combined report for monthly and locked-in March: MAE of expected amount of all customers, MAE of buyers, actual consumption coverage of Top 20 selected each month, total deviation and group deviation of Top 20; AP, Brier of purchase probability, deviation of predicted number of people; deviation of conditional median buyer MAE and amount. Month-by-month errors and deviations are reported for the three test months and cannot be substituted for stability by pooled offsets. Save sources, purge exclusions, amount vs. snapshot tag reconciliation, latest training/calibration tag cutoffs per fold, and script/protocol hashes.
- **Expected amount advancement conditions**: Relative to the historical 180 day amount baseline, combined MAE is at least 10% lower, Top 20 covers at least 3 higher percentage points, the combined total deviation is within ± 5%, and the Top 20 group deviation is within ± 10%; three test months MAE and Top 20 must not be worse than the baseline.
- **Conditional Amount Advance Condition**: The combined buyer MAE is at least 10% lower than both fixed controls, and at least two of the three test months are no worse than either control. This condition is evaluated separately and in no way replaces the "unconditional expected amount of MAE on actual purchasers" in the original enhancement target. Purchasers of the original reinforcement target MAE at least as low as 5% are also listed as is.
- If all conditions are met, it only means that two methods have migration evidence in this independent merchant. This data is global supply chain sales, which may include corporate or physical channels. The amount unit cannot be mixed with the original British online merchant; regardless of whether it is passed or not, it cannot replace the original merchant's same-store acceptance in the new month. Do not integrate consumption forecasts into the dashboard and do not claim certain marketing revenue before there is credible acceptance by the same store.
