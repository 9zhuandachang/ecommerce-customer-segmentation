## Judgment

The current model is suitable for continuing to study the amount ranking of "which customers deserve to be reached first"; a single unconditional amount score cannot yet simultaneously prove that the individual amount is accurate, the buyer's amount is accurate, and the monthly total is stable. The ranking of the four groups of external merchants is different from the error return, so we cannot just select the best group, nor can it replace the verification of the original British merchants in the new month. The original merchant's transaction file ends at 2011-12-09. There is currently no later real data from the same merchant, and an independent new month cannot be created.

## Next research sequence

1. Pauses the search for a loss function, stratification threshold, or magnification for point estimates along the viewed month. The first round of diagnosis has been completed for the amount range and high-value customer list; although the double training weight of buyers reduces the buyer error, it greatly harms the majority of zero-spending customers. The new transaction composition feature only slightly improves the list coverage; the order amount change feature only brings small development period benefits to the conditional amount. The calendar month characteristic improves the amount of buyers in some periods, but causes significant regression in other indicators. CDNOW once again has the conflict of "all MAE improves, buyers MAE worsens, and monthly totals swing". The decomposition of the number of orders in the development period shows that the number of early orders is overestimated; repurchase probability calibration can improve the overall error and total deviation, but it continues to hurt the buyer's amount error. 0.5 hard gating is further evidence that single-value predictions that only optimize the entire MAE will harm buyers and high-value lists. The next study should use "estimated consumption amount" and "typical purchase amount" as two clearly labeled outputs, and evaluate their applicable indicators separately, while retaining the original reinforcement target comparison. The best indicators of the two outputs cannot be combined into a passing result. DataCo's natural time series sources are not trustworthy enough, and Olist's fixed test buyers are insufficient; no development optimization will be promoted to an acceptance conclusion before a new true independent time series that meets the sample size is found. The fitting rate cannot be around the already viewed CDNOW 4 – 6 month or the original project September-November.
2. The original challenge goal and unreached projects are still displayed; if there is new data in the future, the final plan will be locked and independently accepted by the same merchant. The customer sampling interval and monthly stability are stated separately.

Before credible acceptance, the consumption forecast is still an offline experiment and will not be used as a formal customer amount estimate in the dashboard.

## Data conditions required for independent acceptance

Based on the current 180 -day historical input, 30 -day future tags and the beginning of the month cut-off definition, an executable new verification scheme for the same merchant is to obtain continuous real transactions from **2011-12-10 to at least 2012-03-31**, and retain customer IDs and orders that can be identified across periods Fields required for ID, order time, product/amount calculation. After freezing the model and cleaning rules, you can use the 2012 years, 1, 2, and 3 months as three new complete future 30 day inspection months, and lock the source hash and unified acceptance threshold before viewing the tags. Other new sources must first undergo equivalent identity, time coverage, label completeness and sample size audits. There is currently no such data; continuing to search for models or magnifications for existing months is not a substitute for independent acceptance.
