# Development of customer group uncertainty during the confirmation period

There are 8,146 customer monthly snapshots and 3,093 customers in 2011, June-August. Resample by customer 500 times, random seed 42, all methods share the customers drawn each time.
This is a sample of customers over a fixed three-month period and does not include changes in future months and is not a new independent test.

The interval only checks how much changes in customer composition will fluctuate the conclusion in these viewed months. Even if the entire range is above zero, that does not prove that a new month or another merchant will meet the original challenge criteria.
