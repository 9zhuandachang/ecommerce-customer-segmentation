# Olist Anonymous e-commerce orders: fixed double output cross-merchant inspection before downloading

Recorded before downloading the full source file and viewing the 30 days ahead label and any model results for this data. The source is [Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce) self-published by Olist, which publicly states that this is approximately 10 anonymized real commercial orders in 2016 – 2018 year, and the license is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The original compressed package is only placed in the temporary directory of the local system; the warehouse only retains the aggregation results, source URL, SHA- 256, date and exclusion audit, and does not save customer-level external predictions or original rows.

## Target, source fields and fixed time segmentation

- Only read the order ID, order customer ID, order time and status of `olist_orders_dataset.csv`; the order customer ID of `olist_customers_dataset.csv`, stable `customer_unique_id`; the order ID, order line ID, product ID and `price` of `olist_order_items_dataset.csv`. Does not read addresses, zip codes, names, reviews or geographic locations. Check the primary key and associated coverage; multiple product lines in the same order are each calculated as an amount, and the invoice frequency is based on the unique order ID. Each order with a valid product line must belong to only one stable customer.
- The amount is defined as the **total price of the ordered goods**, excluding freight and refunds; only the product lines with valid customers, orders, time, products and positive prices are retained, and the prices are checked by points. To avoid filtering historical inputs with final fulfillment status that is not known at the time of forecasting, do not filter by order final `order_status`; order and amount distributions are reported by status, making it clear that the target is order GMV, not settled net revenue. Both customer snapshots and future tags use the same definition.
- Create 1 days for each month with 180 days input `[t-180d,t)` and 30 days for future `[t,t+30d)` tags. Only use complete coverage of 2017 - 04 to 2018 - 08 cut-off points; if the original file cannot cover 2017-04-01 before 180 days or 2018-08-01 after 30 days, stop evaluation. 2017 - 04 to 2018 - 02 are the historical training period, 2018 - 03 to 05 are development confirmations, **2018 - 06 to 08 are the last three months of this lockdown**. Only earlier cutoff points for which the label window has completely ended are used for each forecast day. Fixed truncation at 2018 - 08, not using 2018 - 09 / 10 month cutoff points near the end of the file. If any lock-in month has less than 1,000 snapshots of customers with orders in the previous 180 days or less than 100 customers with future positive-amount orders, the feasibility is reported without judging the model.

## Fixed candidates and two outputs

1. **Full customer expected amount**: Reuse the fixed combination of `calibrated_hurdle_protocol.md`. The purchase probability is generated by the stage five A group 8 features LightGBM ( 15 leaf, 64 tree, 0.05 learning rate, class balance, seed 42 ); each fold is fitted in the earlier maturity month, the last three maturity months are reserved for independent calibration, and the most recent two maturity months are used as the sigmoid probability calibration of the frozen classifier. The post-purchase mean uses Gamma LightGBM with 20 features before the cutoff point, trained only on mature historical buyers ( 120 trees, 0.05, 15 leaves, seeds 42 ). Multiply the two to get the estimated consumption amount. The comparison is the same batch of customer history 180 days' commodity amount `/6` and fixed 20 features Tweedie ( 120 tree, 0.05, 15 leaves, power 1.5, seed 42 ). No actual future purchase status or any multiples based on the results of this data are used.
2. **If the amount after purchase**: 20 feature L 1 LightGBM conditional median, only fitted on mature historical buyers ( 120 tree, 0.05, 15 leaves, seeds 42 ); only evaluated for customers who actually have positive orders in the future. The fixed comparison is the historical 180 day amount `/6` and the historical 180 day average order amount. This value can only be interpreted as the "amount if purchased" and cannot be mixed with the expected amount for the entire customer.

## Pre-Committed Reports and Judgments

- Combined report for each month and March: All MAE, **unconditional amount** of actual buyers MAE, Top 20 actual product amount coverage, total total deviation, Top 20 group deviation; purchase probability AP, Brier and predicted buyer number deviation; conditional median buyer MAE and amount deviation. Top 20 are selected each month. Report the zero amount ratio for each month, the latest mature tag cutoff for training/calibration, and check the tag and product line amounts. USD/R$/GBP do not consolidate absolute amounts.
- The original enhanced challenge goals are as follows: relative to the historical 180 day amount baseline, all MAE merged in March will be reduced by at least 10%, unconditional purchasers MAE will be reduced by at least 5%, Top 20 coverage will be increased by at least 3 percentage points, total deviation ± 5%, Top 20 group deviation ± 10%, three months of testing, MAE and Top 20 are not bad.
- In addition, the two business outputs are judged separately: the expected amount meets all MAE, Top 20, total and monthly conditions in the above goals; the median buyer MAE of the conditions is at least 10% lower than both **fixed controls, and at least in the 2 / 3 test month, it is not worse than the two. This is a supplementary judgment to explain the purpose of the two values ​​and does not replace the original enhanced challenge goal.
- The source merchant, user behavior, currency, and price definition are different from the original British merchant; even if the external test is improved, it can only be used as evidence of cross-merchant migration, and cannot be called the acceptance of the original project by the same merchant in the new month. When there is no such real data from the same merchant, the amount prediction is still not connected to the dashboard.
